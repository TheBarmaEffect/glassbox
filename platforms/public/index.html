GlassBox Platform Gateway

User-invoked reasoning audits for AI answers. GlassBox turns an explicitly selected question/answer pair into a compact Trust Card with claim reasoning, ECS dimensions, adversarial probes, constitutional checks, and a deterministic audit reference.

Available as private-by-default interactions for Discord, Slack, Telegram, GitHub, Reddit pilots, and approved server-side integrations.

GlassBox is an automated reasoning review, not a fact-check, moderation decision, or professional advice.
