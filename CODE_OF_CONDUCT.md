# Code of Conduct

Glassbox follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- **Be welcoming.** Treat new contributors as smart people who don't yet know our conventions. They're not the convention's fault, they're an opportunity to improve the docs.
- **Stay on the substance.** Critique code, not contributors. *"This adds a coupling between the red-team engine and the Trust Card builder"* is fine. *"You don't get how MCP works"* is not.
- **No harassment.** Sexist, racist, transphobic, ableist, anti-religious, or otherwise demeaning comments are not allowed in any project space — issues, PRs, discussions, social channels, conferences. We will remove your contributions and block you. No second strike.
- **Stay focused.** This is a runtime AI verification project. Off-topic political content in PRs gets the same treatment as off-topic anything else: closed politely with a pointer to a more appropriate venue.

### Reporting

Contact **Karthik Barma** at `thebarmaeffect@gmail.com` for anything you'd rather not put in a public issue. Reports are confidential.

### Maintainer commitments

- We respond to reports within 7 days.
- We don't tolerate retaliation against people who report in good faith.
- We apply the same standards to ourselves as we apply to everyone else.
